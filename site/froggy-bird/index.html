froggy-bird

The Anuran Field Simulator. Pilot a specimen along the wetland transect; every reed cleared logs one observation-hour. Click, tap, or press Space to flap. The heron is not a bug. fly, fool.

A standalone canvas module with zero dependencies, extracted from the frog research institute over at isitbensbirthday.com — one game, one repo, two sites. source.
