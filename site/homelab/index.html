Built from parts, running 24/7 a few feet from the workbench it was assembled on. The stuff I couldn't replace also leaves the house every night, encrypted.

what it runs

Every app on the dashboard is a container on this box, alongside the media server and the smart-home brain. The headliners:

$ docker compose ps · condensed
namestatus
media-serverUp 5 weeks
home-automationUp 5 weeks (healthy)
home-hqUp 3 days
game-stationUp 2 weeks
card-binderUp 6 days
frog-rainUp forever (do not stop)
…and other thingsUp

House rules: the firewall denies by default, versions are pinned, and nothing inside the house listens to the internet.

it also does

The same box watches the house: thermostats, the washer and dryer, the car's charge, and the cat's heated blanket. The cockpit for all of it is home-hq; the real one never leaves the house, but there's a full simulation with canned data. The rest of the basement is a workbench: a 3D printer, a retro-console Raspberry Pi, and the spot where new projects earn their way onto the server.
